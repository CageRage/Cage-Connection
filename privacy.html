Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

Cage Connection is a multiplayer movie-trivia game operated by Rick Duda under the registered messaging brand Rick Duda A2P Messaging Bundle. Questions about this policy can be sent to rick.duda@gmail.com.

Information we collect

When you play, the game may process your player name, room code, game actions, scores, chat messages, and technical data needed to operate a shared game. If you voluntarily sign up for SMS alerts, we also collect your US mobile number, your SMS consent status and relevant consent and messaging records.

How we use information

We use game information to run multiplayer sessions, synchronize gameplay, and respond to support requests. For players who expressly opt in, we use mobile numbers solely to send non-marketing Cage Connection messages when it is their turn, when a connection is challenged, and for necessary subscription confirmations or service responses. Message frequency varies with gameplay. Message and data rates may apply.

Service providers and sharing

We use technical service providers as needed to operate the game and deliver SMS, including Firebase/Google Cloud for game infrastructure and Twilio for messaging. They process information to provide those services, not to market to players on our behalf. We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Mobile numbers and messaging consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

SMS choices

SMS participation is optional and is not required to play. You can withdraw consent at any time by replying STOP to a Cage Connection message. For assistance, reply HELP or email rick.duda@gmail.com. Opting out of texts does not prevent you from playing the game.

Security, retention and access

We limit access to mobile numbers and messaging records to what is needed to operate and support alerts. We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for the service, support, compliance, or applicable legal requirements. To request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, email rick.duda@gmail.com.

Updates

We may update this policy as the game develops. A revised effective date will appear on this page.
