Cage Connection — Game Rules

The objective

Build a network of movies connected through performers they have in common. Your goal is to earn points while creating valid links between the movies on the board. The player scores and Board score are shown separately.

Taking a turn

  1. Add Movie: choose a movie to place on the board.
  2. Connect: select two movies and identify an actor appearing in both. A direct, shared-actor connection earns 1 point.
  3. Bridge connection: connect two movies through another film and its performers. The game awards 2 points for a bridge connection; its cast validation is not automatic.
  4. Bonus Round: choose a difficulty worth 1, 3, or 5 points and a category. A correct answer earns its point value; a wrong answer subtracts its point value. A hint reduces the points awarded for a correct answer by 1.
  5. Skip: pass your turn. When playing online, the separate timed skip option may be available after the configured wait.

Scoring and challenges

Player score: points earned for your moves, Bonus Round results, and relevant penalties. When a connection joins separate groups into the main continuous chain, an additional merge bonus may apply.

Board score: totals the connection values on the shared board (1 for a shared actor, 2 for a bridge, plus applicable merge bonuses), less any recorded board penalties. It is not another player's score.

Challenge: select a connection you believe is invalid. A successful challenge removes the connection and costs the challenged player twice its point value; an unsuccessful challenge costs the challenger 3 points. Follow the on-screen confirmation and verification steps.

Tools & cheats

Cast Lookup (cheat)
Click a movie to see its stored cast. Costs 2 points; opening its Wikipedia cast page costs 3 additional points (5 total).
Add / Remove Cast Member
Correct the game's locally stored cast information. Changes are saved on your current device; they are not automatically verified facts for other players.
Find Path
Shows the shortest route between two movies using connections already on the board.
Show Index
Lists movies and actors represented in the current game's connections.
View
Switch between Board and List views or change the size of the movie cards.
Find Movie / Clear Selection / Inspect Links
Locate a movie, cancel an active selection, or inspect existing connections.
Remove Movie
Removes a movie and the connections attached to it after confirmation.
Reset Game / Start Fresh
Clears the board and game progress after confirmation. Use with care in a shared online game.

Bonus Round categories

True or False, Multiple Choice, Photo Identification, Movie Quote, Cage Quote, and Cage Fact. Questions come from the current game question bank. A question is not reused within the same game once marked used.

The game itself is the authority on scores and turn eligibility if a situation differs from this quick guide. Challenge verification and manual overrides may be necessary when cast records are incomplete.
